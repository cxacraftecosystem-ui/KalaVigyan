Principles

Nine rules decide most questions. The tokens and components below are how they look.

  1. 1

    One control per job

    Each task has one way to do it on a screen. A button that repeats a link, two ways to change one value, or a stepper beside a button is a defect.

  2. 2

    Button rows follow the ActionGroup rule

    One action is centred. Two sit on one line, sharing the width on a phone. Three or more stack full width on a phone and become pills from 640px. A destructive action goes last, set apart.

  3. 3

    Concise copy

    Sentence case, short labels, verbs on buttons, the finding in a chart title. No marketing adjectives, no exclamation marks, no instructions the layout already gives.

  4. 4

    Citations quiet but always present

    Every reader sentence carries a cluster of indigo numerals that opens the verbatim quote, its locator and its reliability. The cluster never breaks the line or changes its height.

  5. 5

    Never admit a gap

    No screen says not built yet, coming soon, no data or not enough evidence. A craft without a monograph shows its plate, its record and questions that start research. An empty list tells the reader how to fill it.

  6. 6

    Motion only to explain

    Movement shows a reaction or a process: the dip, the cloth turning blue, a numeral pressed. Nothing drifts, staggers, decorates or follows the scroll. Every motion has a still end state, authored as the default style.

  7. 7

    Indigo means you can press it

    Indigo is the only interactive colour. Madder means emphasis, disagreement or real hazard; terracotta means place; turmeric only highlights. Data never borrows any of them: every ramp step, series and family sits 10 or more delta E away.

  8. 8

    Names in their own scripts, at equal standing

    An Indian name is set at the same size as its English one, and only where a source states it and names this craft, not its technique in general. Until then the plate carries the English name, never a guess.

  9. 9

    Pictures are plates, credited where they appear

    Caption first, credit after, standard thumbnail widths only, no repeats on a page, and no stock, shopping or AI imagery.

Specimen colours

Depicted materials. They do not change with the theme, are always framed and textured, and are never used for a control. Every dyed blue is chroma 0.06 or less and the interactive indigo 0.15 or more, so a dye never reads as a control.

Every specimen swatch sits in a 1px ink-2 frame with a 2px raised mat inside it, so its edge reads at 3:1 or better against the mat or the frame in every theme (frame in each specimen records both ratios). Specimens depict real dyes, so dye-indigo and the dips sit 7 to 10 delta E from indigo-hover and dye-madder sits near the high-contrast madder. Safeguards: a specimen is always textured with the weave, framed and named in a key; it never fills a control, a link or a chart series; the interactive indigo is chroma 0.15 or more and every dyed blue chroma 0.06 or less.

Data and maps

Ramps for meaning

Each variable gets a ramp that means something, in light, dark and both high-contrast themes. Sequential ramps flip lightness in dark but keep their hues tied to the value. Every step sits 10 or more ΔE from indigo, madder, terracotta and turmeric, and every first step 10 or more from ground, land and water; the checks below are the generator’s.

Light CVD ΔE 12.2 · normal ΔE 24.4 · reserved 11.6Dark CVD ΔE 12.0 · normal ΔE 16.1 · reserved 13.9HC light CVD ΔE 12.0 · normal ΔE 24.3 · reserved 11.0HC dark CVD ΔE 12.0 · normal ΔE 16.0 · reserved 13.5

OKLab delta E x100, protan and deutan simulated with Machado 2009 at severity 1.0, adjacent pairs. Target 8, floor 6; normal vision floor 15. Every family hue sits 10 or more from the reserved colours. Fixed order, never cycled. Bars, stacks and legends may use all eight. Maps, scatter and small multiples carry at most three families at once (all-pairs check); fold the rest into More crafts or facet. Every family mark carries a 1px ink-1 outline (bars, swatches) or sits in an ink-ringed marker, so no family relies on its own contrast. Material-matched hues (brass for metal, terracotta for clay, oxblood for leather) would sit on turmeric, terracotta and madder, and the plate tones are too close in lightness to pass the CVD check; families stay categorical and plates keep the material.

Series colours

  • series-rainRainfall bars and lines, in the rain ramp's teal in every theme.
  • series-heatTemperature lines and dots, in the heat ramp's rose-plum in every theme.
  • series-countCounts of crafts: proportional circles and the ranked bars beside them, in the density ramp's umber.
  • chart-seriesThe default series colour: one neutral series when the variable has no series token of its own.
  • chart-emphThe one mark the title names (the record year, the flagship craft). Nothing else.

A line or bar series never takes a ramp step, because ramps flip in dark. Each variable has a series token that keeps its hue in every theme; anything else takes chart-series, and only the mark the title names takes chart-emph.

Five patterns, one meaning each.

Type

Newsreader for the voice, IBM Plex Sans for the interface, IBM Plex Mono for codes, Noto Serif in each Indian script. Every size is a named rem token, fluid between 360 and 1440px, display sizes to 1920px.

--font-headline

Kutch Ajrakh, sourced

Display lines only: hero, title, h1, h2, numerals, stats. A separate token from font-display, which stays on the UI face. font-optical-sizing: auto. Newsreader's opsz axis runs 6 to 72, so auto gives opsz 72 to every size from 72px up and follows the size below; never force opsz 72 below 40px.

--font-reading

Cloth lifted from the vat is green at first, 1981–2025

Prose, ledes, quotes, captions, h3, names on plates. Old-style proportional figures. The stack routes each Indian script to its Noto Serif by glyph.

--font-ui

Request access · 461 crafts

Everything you operate or scan: nav, buttons, labels, chips, tables, axes, credits, citation numerals, h4. 400, 500, 600, italic 400; b and strong are 600 and font-synthesis is none, so nothing renders faux bold

--font-code

The six-box sign-in code, API keys, PubChem CIDs, slugs. Lining tabular figures; never old-style serif figures.

Rules
  • One cluster a sentence, de-duplicated by source and sorted: 1,4,5. No pills, borders or icons.
  • The cluster sits at max(0.6875rem, 0.62em), raised 0.6em. Its hit area is 24px wide and stays inside its own line (44px wide on touch); a cluster is inline text, so WCAG 2.5.8 holds by its inline exception. Line height never changes.
  • Its accessible name is short, “Sources 1 and 2”, with the titles in aria-description; aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-controls and aria-expanded say what it opens.
  • Margin notes are numbered in ink-3, not indigo: the numeral in the sentence already opens the source, so the margin is read, not pressed. Each note sits beside the first sentence in its chapter that cites it, pushes down on collision and never repeats in a chapter.
  • The popover groups quotes by source, highlights the sentence in turmeric wash, and shows locator and an A to D reliability mark.
  • From 1200px it docks into the margin beside the sentence as a non-modal dialog; focus moves into it and Escape returns it. Below 640px it is a modal bottom sheet with a focus trap.
  • Numbering is per page, in order of first appearance, and stays the same across a page’s chapters.

Chart grammar, with a real chart

  • The title states the finding; the dek gives the method; the source line sits under the chart.
  • Axes in UI 12, tabular lining figures, ink-3; gridlines in chart-grid; the baseline in ink.
  • Direct labels at line ends instead of a legend for one or two series; a legend for three or more.
  • The mark the title names is chart-emph; a series takes its variable’s series token (series-rain, series-heat, series-count) or chart-series, never a ramp step.
  • One tab stop per figure. Arrow keys move between marks, Home and End jump to the ends, the tooltip follows focus and Escape closes it; the table behind “All N values” is the full keyboard and screen-reader path.
  • Charts title their finding; diagrams (Blue, Red, the vat) take a descriptive title.
  • One axis per chart. Uncertainty as a band or whisker, never a lone number.
