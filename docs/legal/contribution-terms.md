# Contribution terms (version 2026-11-19)

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-19. In force for every contribution made where this version is
shown at the moment of contributing. A change to the text is a new version, shown and accepted
again.

## What these terms cover

Anything you give KalaVigyan: facts with their quotes and sources, pictures, drawings and AI-made
illustrations, interactive components, page layouts, and corrections. They apply whether you
contribute on the site, through the Knowledge API or through the MCP server.

## What you give the Centre

You give the Centre of Excellence for Unified AI-Enabled Craft Ecosystem Platform, IIT Kharagpur, a
worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual and irrevocable licence to publish, edit, translate, adapt,
combine and share your contribution, including under open licences, in any medium.

- **No public credit.** Your contribution is published without your name. Who contributed what is
  kept privately, for moderation and audit only.
- **Consent cannot be revoked.** Once your contribution is approved and published, it stays. You can
  withdraw it while it is still waiting for review.

## What you confirm

- You hold the right to give it: it is your own work, or its owner has allowed it.
- Quotes are copied exactly from the source you name, with where they appear in it.
- It names no private person, and shows no person's likeness, without their consent.
- It shares no knowledge a community holds as restricted or sacred without that community's
  permission.
- If an AI tool made any part of it, you have said so, named the tool and given the prompt. An
  AI-made picture is always shown as an illustration, never as a photograph.

## What happens next

- Every contribution passes our automatic checks: quotes are verified against the source, sources
  are graded, and repeats are refused.
- A contribution from a Professor, Admin or Master Admin is published at once. Anyone else's waits
  for an Admin, who may approve, edit or reject it.
- We may correct, move or retire published content when the evidence changes. Retired content is
  kept in the page's history.

## How this document is kept true

This is the approved text of version 2026-11-19. `CONTRIBUTION_TERMS_VERSION` in
`backend/app/research/own_proposals.py` names the version, and
`backend/tests/contributions/test_own_proposals_rules.py` holds this file's heading to it; the site
shows its own copy from `frontend/lib/ownProposalsContract.ts`. A change to the text is a new
version: change this file, the site's copy and the version together, with the owner's approval.
