# Image declaration (version 2026-11-19)

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-19. This is the statement a contributor
confirms when giving a picture (`termsVersion: "2026-11-19"` in `ImageDeclaration`,
`docs/contracts/image-contributions.md`). It is shown above the confirm box.

> I took or made this picture, or I hold the right to share it. I give the Centre of Excellence for
> Unified AI-Enabled Craft Ecosystem Platform, IIT Kharagpur, the right to publish it under the
> contribution terms, without my name. Once it is approved and published, this consent cannot be
> revoked.
>
> If an AI tool made it, I have named the tool and model and given the prompt, and I understand it
> will be shown as an illustration, never as a photograph, and only beside the facts it shows.
>
> It shows no real person's face or name without their consent, and nothing a community keeps
> restricted or sacred.

## How this document is kept true

This is the approved text of version 2026-11-19, the `termsVersion` of `ImageDeclaration`. The site
shows its own copy from `frontend/lib/imageContributionContract.ts`; nothing compares the two, so
compare them whenever either changes. A change to the text is a new version, with the owner's
approval.
